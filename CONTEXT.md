# CONTEXT — where dhancha sits

FAKIR (Foundational Architecture for Knowledge, Intelligence & Reasoning) spans the
ISIC x ISCO x ISCED lattice crossed with AGI layers L0-L9 and the ILM language axis.
Each point of that lattice can become a domain. dhancha is what a domain must satisfy
to be one.

- **FAKIR** supplies the lattice and the seeding gesture (click a point, seed a task).
- **dhancha** supplies the shape the seeded task must produce.
- **PANINI** supplies the language the work is expressed in (`cyclers/*.pni`).
- **MEZ** mounts the result; it does not own it. Every domain is sovereign — it owns its
  ontology, runtime, artifacts, evolution and users, and exists without Mez.
- **Candor / Tok DOI / spiguard / Misty** seal, clear, attest and mint, in that order.

dhancha is a peer of the domains, not their container.
